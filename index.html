How it works

Every step, permanently recorded

Four checkpoints from harvest to consumer — each one tamper-proof on Ethereum.

🌱 Farm Registration
Farmers register batches with harvest date, origin, and initial quality — permanently stored. Each batch gets a unique on-chain ID.
🚚 Logistics Updates
Transporters and warehouses append data about each batch — vehicle name, warehouse facility, temperatures and timestamps.
📱 Consumer Scan
Customers scan a QR code to see the entire on-chain journey from farmer to market. No wallet or app required — just scan and read.

Built for transparency

VerdantChain uses Ethereum smart contracts to immutably record each step of fresh produce logistics. The system tracks and ensures the movement of fruit batches from the farm to the final consumer by utilizing smart contracts and blockchain technology. Every transaction is permanently documented and accessible via QR code scanning — no MetaMask needed to view.
