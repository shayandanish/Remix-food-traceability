Built for transparency

VerdantChain uses smart contracts to immutably record each step of fresh produce logistics. . The system will track and ensure the movement of fruit batches from the farm to the final consumers by utilizing smart contracts and blockchain technology. In order to empower stakeholders and increase consumer trust, every transaction will be permanently documented in the blockchain and made available via QR code scanning.
